Athlete + Family Questions

Straight answers before you commit.

Who is this program for?

High-school recruits, JUCO players and college athletes entering or already active in the transfer portal who want an organized, proactive recruiting strategy.

Do you guarantee an offer or scholarship?

No. We cannot control coaches, roster needs, admissions or scholarship decisions. We improve strategy, presentation, targeting and consistency.

Is there a minimum commitment?

The Monthly Flex Program has no minimum commitment. The 90-day, six-month and twelve-month programs are one-time program purchases designed for athletes who want a longer recruiting or development roadmap.

Do you contact coaches for the athlete?

We help the athlete create and execute effective outreach with hands-on guidance. The exact support level is discussed during evaluation.

What happens after I apply?

We review the information, determine whether the program is a fit and contact the athlete or family with next steps.
